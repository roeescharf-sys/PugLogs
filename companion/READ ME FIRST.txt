PugLogs - Warcraft Logs for your Mythic+ applicants
https://github.com/roeescharf-sys/PugLogs

STARTING IT
  Windows: double-click PugLogs.exe
           If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  macOS:   double-click PugLogs
           If macOS says it can't be opened: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
           scroll down and click "Open Anyway" (or right-click PugLogs > Open).
           If it then complains about other files, open Terminal and run once:
             xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine <drag the PugLogs folder here>

THE FIRST TIME
  1. PugLogs installs the WoW addon for you and opens a page in your browser.
  2. That page shows how to create your free Warcraft Logs API client (about a minute)
     and where to paste it.
  3. In WoW, type /reload.

EVERY TIME YOU PUG
  Start PugLogs, keep its browser page next to WoW (play in windowed mode), list your key.
  Hover applicants after clicking "Paste logs" in the Group Finder. In game: /pl for commands.

Keep the PugLogs window open while you play; close it to stop PugLogs.
