PugLogs - Warcraft Logs for your Mythic+ applicants
https://github.com/roeescharf-sys/PugLogs

INSTALLING
  macOS:   drag PugLogs onto the Applications folder next to it, then open it from Applications.
           If macOS says it can't be opened: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
           click "Open Anyway" (or right-click PugLogs > Open). The first start can take up to
           half a minute.
  Windows: the easiest is PugLogs-Setup.exe from the download page. From this zip instead:
           double-click PugLogs.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click
           "More info", then "Run anyway".

THE FIRST TIME
  1. PugLogs installs the WoW addon for you (if you don't have it from CurseForge already)
     and opens the PugLogs window.
  2. The window shows how to create your free Warcraft Logs API client (about a minute)
     and where to paste it.
  3. In WoW, type /reload.

EVERY TIME YOU PUG
  Start PugLogs and list your key. Keep its window next to WoW, or tick "keep on top"
  to float it over the game (play WoW in windowed mode).
  Hover applicants after clicking "Paste logs" in the Group Finder. In game: /pl for commands.

Keep the PugLogs window open while you play; closing it stops PugLogs.
PugLogs tells you in its window when a new version is available.
